# Accommodation and Ground Transport Playbook

Research checked on 2026-05-30.

## Accommodation Search Stack

Use multiple sources because hotel search is a swamp with nicer fonts:

1. Google Hotels for map scanning, neighborhood comparison, and quick rate sanity checks.
2. Booking.com / Hotels.com for refundable inventory and review volume.
3. Direct hotel sites for final rate, cancellation rules, loyalty perks, and breakfast details.
4. Hostelworld only if shared/private hostel rooms are acceptable for that trip.
5. Airbnb only when apartments meaningfully beat hotels and cleaning/service fees are visible.

## Accommodation Rules

- Prefer refundable rates until transport and concert tickets are confirmed.
- Track total stay price, not nightly headline rate.
- Record city tax, breakfast, cancellation deadline, and whether payment is immediate.
- Score location by door-to-door time to venue and airport/station, not neighborhood vibes alone.
- For Amsterdam, book earlier or accept a bigger lodging estimate. It is very good at punishing optimism.

## Ground Transport Search Stack

- Rail: operator sites first where practical, then Trainline/Rail Europe/Omio/Rome2Rio for discovery.
- Coach/bus: FlixBus, National Express, Megabus where relevant.
- Local transit: official city transport sites or visitor pass pages for day-ticket estimates.
- Airport transfers: airport official pages, city transit authority, and Google Maps sanity checks.

## Cost Categories To Track

- Intercity transport: flight, train, coach, ferry, car hire if ever relevant.
- Accommodation: total stay cost, cancellation, taxes.
- Event tickets: primary ticket price plus fees where visible.
- Local transit: airport transfer, city transit, late-night taxi risk.
- Food/drink: daily estimate by city and trip style.
- Buffer: luggage storage, lockers, card fees, missed-train stupidity, and other little bites.

## Booking Guidance

- Flights and event tickets are usually the irreversible anchors.
- Accommodation should generally be refundable until anchors are confirmed.
- Coaches are useful as a fallback price floor, but very long overnight bus trips should be penalized heavily.
- Rail wins when it is city-centre to city-centre and avoids airport transfer grief.
